About Us

Welcome to ApaniNewz — the official blogging and news platform for LJ University students and staff. This space is designed to share real stories, campus updates, achievements, and creative ideas from within the LJ community.

At ApaniNewz, we believe every voice matters. Whether it's academic success, event highlights, project showcases, or personal reflections — this platform provides a space for meaningful and inspiring content.

Who Can Contribute?
Any student, faculty member, or staff at LJ University is welcome to contribute. No matter your background or department, your insights are valuable.

How to Submit?
Click on the "Submit Article" or "Share News" button, complete the form, and upload your content. Our editorial team will review and publish it after approval.

Join us in making ApaniNewz a platform that represents the voice of LJ University — honest, diverse, and creative.
